# Lamp & Lyric Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Lamp & Lyric is a private, offline-first song library and practice reader published under the Lampstone Studios developer name. You can use its core features without creating an account.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores songs you create or import, song edits, favorites, tags, setlists, practice and display preferences, viewing history, and reference-track information in private app storage. Local reference-track files remain on your device. The app does not upload local audio or MIDI files.

If Android system backup is enabled, Android may back up a compact personal-data snapshot to your Google account for device replacement or reinstall recovery. That snapshot includes My Songs, current custom edits, favorites, tags, ordered setlists, synchronizable preferences, and web reference-track links. It excludes imported songbook originals, full edit history, viewing history, diagnostics, caches, local file links, and local audio or MIDI files.

You can also create a manual personal backup and choose where to save or share it. Lamp & Lyric does not control copies you export.

## Optional account and cross-device sync

If you choose to create or use an account, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address or Google sign-in credential and assigns an account identifier. Lamp & Lyric stores your personal layer in Google Cloud Firestore under that identifier so it can sync across your devices.

The synchronized personal layer includes My Songs, current custom edits, favorites, tags, ordered setlists, synchronizable preferences, web reference-track links, and deletion records needed for synchronization. Imported songbook originals, full edit history, viewing history, local file links, and local audio or MIDI files are not synchronized.

Lamp & Lyric does not provide public profiles or share song content with other users.

## Reference tracks and external services

When you play a direct audio URL, your device connects to the host of that URL, which may receive your IP address and request metadata under its own privacy policy. External links open in the provider's app or website. Lamp & Lyric does not download or extract media from those services.

## Analytics, advertising, and sales

Lamp & Lyric does not include Firebase Analytics, show advertising, request advertising-ID access, sell personal data, or use personal song content for advertising.

## Data deletion

You can delete local data from **Settings > Danger Zone**. When signed in, **Delete account and data** deletes the synchronized Firestore data, Firebase Authentication account, local app data, and the current automatic-backup snapshot. Firebase may require you to sign in again before it permits account deletion.

You can also request account deletion while signed out through the [account-deletion page](/lampandlyric/delete-account/). Uninstalling the app removes local data, subject to any Android backup retained under your Google account settings.

## Service providers and security

Lamp & Lyric uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for optional account sync, and Android's system backup for optional device backup. Data is transmitted using the security provided by those services. Firestore rules restrict each signed-in user to their own data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children

Lamp & Lyric is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13.
