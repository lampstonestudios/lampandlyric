# Request Lamp & Lyric Account Deletion

Lampstone Studios is the developer name for Lamp & Lyric. If you can no longer access the app, email [lampandlyric.app@gmail.com](mailto:lampandlyric.app@gmail.com) from the address associated with your Lamp & Lyric account.

Please include **Lamp & Lyric account deletion request** in the subject. Do not include your password or other sensitive information.

We will use the request to identify and delete the associated Firebase Authentication account and synchronized personal data in Cloud Firestore. Local data on devices is controlled by Android and can be removed from **Settings > Danger Zone** or by uninstalling the app. Android backup copies are controlled by your Google account and device backup settings.

If you can access the app, the fastest path is **Settings > Danger Zone > Delete account and data**.

For general support or privacy questions, use the same email address.
